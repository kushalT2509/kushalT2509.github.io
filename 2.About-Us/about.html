About Animal Awareness

Who We Are

Animal Awareness is an educational website created to provide reliable and engaging information about animals from around the world. The website introduces visitors to wildlife, endangered species, responsible pet ownership, and conservation efforts while encouraging respect and appreciation for all living creatures. Through informative content, images, and interactive features, Animal Awareness aims to make learning about animals both enjoyable and meaningful.

Our Mission

Our mission is to educate people about the importance of protecting animals and preserving their natural habitats. We believe that increasing awareness can inspire individuals to make responsible choices, care for pets properly, and support conservation initiatives that help protect wildlife for future generations.

Our Values

  • Promote animal welfare.
  • Encourage responsible pet ownership.
  • Support wildlife conservation.
  • Increase awareness of endangered and non-enfangered species.

Academic Project

Animal Awareness was developed as part of the Secure Front-End Application module at the University of Technology, Mauritius (UTM). The website demonstrates the use of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap, and multimedia elements while providing educational information about animals. Learn more about the programme by visiting the CyberSecurity page.

Thank You

Thank you for visiting Animal Awareness. We hope this website encourages you to learn more about animals, appreciate the incredible diversity of wildlife, and play a part in protecting our planet for future generations.
