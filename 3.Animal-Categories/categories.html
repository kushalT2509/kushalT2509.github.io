Animal Categories

Animals can be grouped into different categories based on their physical characteristics, habitats, and ways of life. Learning about these categories helps us understand the diversity of the animal kingdom and the unique adaptations that allow each species to survive.

Mammals

Mammals are warm-blooded animals that are usually covered with hair or fur and can be found in habitats all around the world, from forests and deserts to oceans. They are known for their intelligence, ability to care for their young, and their wide variety of sizes and behaviours. Familiar mammals include elephants, tigers, dolphins, and monkeys.
